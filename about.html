Crafting Grandeur with Artistry & Precision

Born from an obsession with exquisite detail, AURÈLIA brings together timeless French aesthetics, architectural props, and modern theatrical staging across destination celebrations in India.

THE AURÈLIA PHILOSOPHY

Every Celebration Tells a Story

At AURÈLIA, we believe that luxury lies in the details that guests feel before they even notice them. The soft golden warmth of dimmable 2200K filament marquee letters, the seamless roll of thick knee-high low fog during a couple’s entry, the organic curve of an ivory velvet lounge—these are the subtle masterstrokes that elevate an event into a memory.

What started in 2018 as a boutique collection of bespoke floral arches has grown into India's premier event props and certified stage special effects atelier. Today, our fleet supplies destination weddings in Udaipur, high-profile galas in Mumbai, royal banquets in Jaipur, and beachfront soirées in Goa.

HOW WE OPERATE

The Three Pillars of AURÈLIA
