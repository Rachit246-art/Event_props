Frequently Asked Questions

Are your cold spark machines safe for indoor ballrooms?

Yes, 100%. Our Sparkulars use non-pyrotechnic titanium composite granules with zero smoke, zero odor, and touch-safe fountain temperatures (<35°C), compliant with 5-star hotel fire alarms.

How early does your crew arrive on event day?

Our certified technicians arrive 3 to 4 hours before the ceremony for complete rigging, cabling, DMX testing, and safety rehearsal.

Do you deliver across multiple destination cities?

Yes! We have dedicated fleet transit vehicles operating across Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Delhi NCR, Karnataka, and Goa.
